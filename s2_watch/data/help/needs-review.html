Needs Review

Needs Review is the first tab: everything waiting on you, newest first, so a glance on returning to the screen answers "what needs me now?" without opening a menu. Its tab shows how many, e.g. Needs Review (6).

ItemWhen it appearsButton
New eventAn open event at High concern first heard in the last 24 hours, or one a desktop alert was raised for at Elevated or above (a single news story about a shooting, however urgent, isn't listed) Open event
AI assessment readyOllama finished assessing an event and the assessment waits to be accepted (Local AI)Review assessment
Shift briefing readyS2 Watch drafted a briefing at a scheduled time (Reports and briefings); it is never sent by itselfReview briefing
Single sourceA radio report only one station has sent, with no official confirmation after 2 hours Open event
Possible missA radio message the watch forms didn't keep that the local AI thinks is news Add as report… / Not news
Unsent draftA report or briefing saved in the last 7 days but not marked sentOpen draft

Double-click a row or use its button. An item goes when you act on it: opened, accepted or dismissed, sent, or added. Hide takes it off this list only (it stays everywhere else, and hidden items stay hidden). The chips above the list (New event (2), Possible miss (3)…) show or hide each kind.

Beside the list

Situation now counts the open events in the watch period by concern level (High, Elevated, Guarded, Low: Events explains the levels). Recent notices keeps the last 20 status-bar messages with their times, so one you missed while away isn't lost.

Example. Back from a break: Needs Review (3) lists New event — Flood — Pennsylvania (High — high urgency · confirmed by NWS), AI assessment ready — Power outage — North Platte, NE and Shift briefing ready — Situation Briefing. Open the event, accept the assessment, review the briefing and send it: the list is empty.

Getting there from an alert

Clicking a desktop alert brings S2 Watch forward on the event it was about (several alerts at once: this tab). Clicking the tray icon once brings the window forward.
