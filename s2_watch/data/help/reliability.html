Reliability tools

These help judge how far to trust a report. Each is a prompt for the analyst, never a verdict.

Station track records

Reports → Sender Track Records… shows how each station's newsworthy radio reports have held up. A report counts for the station when an official source confirmed its event, or you marked it Of interest or Follow-up; against it when you marked it Ignored. With 5 or more judged reports the share that held up suggests a grade:

GradeMeansHeld up
Busually reliable85% or more
Cfairly reliable65% or more
Dnot usually reliable40% or more
Eunreliableunder 40%
Fcannot be judgedfewer than 5 judged reports

A is left to official sources. The grade appears beside each report in the event panel and in MAGNET source ratings.

Example. K6NLX has 8 reports; 5 had their events confirmed by NWS, none were ignored: B — usually reliable (5 of 5 judged reports held up). KI6QDB has 5 reports, 1 marked useful: F — too few judged reports (1 of 5 needed).

Keyword noise

Reports → Keyword Noise… lists keywords that flagged reports you then marked Ignored, noisiest first, with how many came from radio and how many from official feeds. Stop using this word stops it making reports news in that category (other words in the same message still can); Use again undoes it.

Example. Infrastructure · outage · 40 reports · 18 ignored (45%) · radio 18 — net check-ins saying “no outages” keep getting flagged. Stopping “outage” for Infrastructure leaves “power outage”, “blackout” and the rest working. By contrast, Climate · hail · feeds 18 means minor official hail reports were ignored: the word is fine, keep it.

Time in message

When a radio report gives its own time, the event panel shows it beside when it was heard, and follow-up timelines use it.

TORNADO TOUCHDOWN AT 1420Z NEAR BIXBY          → 1420Z
152330Z POWER OUT                              → 2330Z on the 15th
POWER OUT SINCE 9:15 AM CDT                    → 1415Z
DA[261016-0544Z]  (MagCommstat stamp)          → 0544Z 16 Oct
STORM EXPECTED AT 1800Z                        → ignored (a forecast)
WARNING UNTIL 1645Z                            → ignored

A time later than when the message was heard is ignored, except just before midnight. Radio lead still uses when reports were heard.

Fixing a location

Each report in the event panel shows where it was placed and a fix location link. Type where it really is: Tulsa, OK, Osage County, OK, 36.06, -95.88 or EM26ab. A town S2 Watch doesn't know can be looked up on OpenStreetMap with a button (nothing is sent unless you press it).

Example. Messages about “BIXBY” land only at the state level, because Bixby isn't in S2 Watch's place list. Fix one report, look up Bixby, OK, tick Remember: wherever a message says BIXBY: every report mentioning Bixby is placed there from then on. Unticked, the fix applies to that one message.
