Focus regions and custom area

Your focus decides what comes first. Configuration → Focus Regions:

  • Region 1 … Region 10 (the MARS / FEMA regions): tick yours.
  • Custom Area…: a radius (10–1500 km) around a town, county, coordinates or grid square: your area of responsibility.
  • All of the U.S. (no focus): everything is treated the same.

Reports and events in a focus region or the custom area are in focus. They sort first in the lists and briefings and alert normally; elsewhere only high-urgency reports alert. The map draws focus regions bold and the custom area as a dashed circle; the window title shows the focus.

Example. A county S2 cell in Tulsa ticks Region 6 and sets a custom area of 150 km around Tulsa, OK. A flood in Tulsa, OK and a wildfire in Arkansas within 150 km both come first; a storm in Iowa still appears, lower down, and only alerts if it is high urgency. The Events tab's Focus only box shows just the focus.

Metrics and the aircraft layer use the regions only.
