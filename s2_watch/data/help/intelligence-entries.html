Intelligence entries

Reports → Add Intelligence Entry… records a summary you are cleared to share, from a source S2 Watch can't read itself: HSIN, a fusion center, an EOC briefing. Entries join events, the map and briefings like other open-source items.

FieldExample
SourceOklahoma Information Fusion Center
Official sourceTicked: its entries confirm events (reliability A), like NWS or USGS
HandlingTLP:CLEAR · Approved for release to MAGNET · Public information (or type another)
When2026-10-23 14:30 (your local time)
Category / UrgencyClimate / Environment · High
WhereTulsa, OK (a town, county, coordinates or grid square; optional)
SummaryFlooding confirmed along the Arkansas River in Tulsa; two shelters open.

Saving needs I confirm this summary is cleared for release to the network ticked. Summarise what is releasable; never paste restricted products.

What happens next. The entry is always kept, with the category and urgency you chose. If radio stations reported the flooding earlier, the entry joins their event and, being official, marks it ✓ Confirmed by Oklahoma Information Fusion Center with the radio lead. In a MAGNET report it appears as [3] Oklahoma Information Fusion Center [A1] — analyst entry, TLP:CLEAR, heard 1430Z 23 Oct.

Reports → Intelligence Entries… lists your entries. Withdraw takes one off the news lists and events (it stays in History).
