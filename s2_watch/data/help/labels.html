Labels and notes

Labels record what you decided about a report or an event. Labelled items stay listed; they build each station's track record and the keyword-noise list.

LabelKeyEffect
★ Of interestIKept at the top: always shown, even past the row limit.
↪ Follow-up createdFAsks for an optional note, such as your report number. Also pinned.
⊘ IgnoredXDimmed; left out of alerts, metrics and briefings.
Clear labelCRemoves the label and its note.
Edit note…NA note without changing the label.

The keys work in the Reports list and the events list under the map when the list has focus (click a row first). Right-click gives the same choices, and on an event they apply to all its reports. The event panel has a Label button.

Example. You send follow-up report S2-2026-117 about a flood event: select it, press F, type S2-2026-117. The Labels column reads Follow-up created · S2-2026-117, and the event's reports count toward their stations' track records as useful.

Labelling a flagged report Ignored is also how S2 Watch learns which keywords cause false alarms: see Keyword Noise in Reliability tools.
