Watch forms

A message is kept when a checked watch form matches it; S2 Watch then decides whether it is news. Messages no form keeps are not saved (the local AI can still list them under Possible Misses).

Configuration → Watch Forms → Edit Watch Forms… lists the forms in three groups:

  • Topics: keywords for each kind of news (Crime, Infrastructure, Climate / Environment…), checked in every application's messages. Each says what it matches, e.g. Keywords: shooting, shooter, shots fired… (+32 more); hover for the full pattern.
  • Whole applications: every message from one application, e.g. All JS8Call messages.
  • Your forms: forms you added or imported.

Tick or untick a form to turn it on or off; double-click to edit; right-click to edit or remove.

Radiological and nuclear incidents are high-urgency Climate / Environment news, alongside chemical spills and hazmat: a radiological release or contamination, a radiation leak or exposure, anything radioactive, a nuclear incident or accident, a dirty bomb, or a meltdown. “Radiation” alone (solar radiation, radiation therapy) isn't.

Example. KG5RKW: @MAGNET PIN RADIATION EM93DN SRS REPORTED AN UNFILTERED RADIOLOGICAL RELEASE AFTER A LAB FIRE… becomes a high-urgency report placed at grid EM93DN (the Savannah River Site, SC).

Adding a form

Add form… asks for a name, which application to watch, and what to keep:

KeepExampleKeepsDoesn't keep
Every messageWatch messages from FLMSGevery FLMSG form—
Messages containing this textSKYWARNW5ABC: SKYWARN NET ACTIVATED W5ABC: WX SPOTTER NET
Messages with any of these wordsbridge out, road closed, washout BRIDGE OUT ON RT 9THE BRIDGE IS OPEN
Regular expression (advanced)\bboil\s+water\bBOIL WATER NOTICE FOR ADA BOILER ROOM

“Any of these words” matches whole words: ice doesn't match “police”. Capital letters never matter.

Narrow a form further with Only this message type (e.g. STATREP for MagCommstat StatReps, EARTHQUAKE for OSINT quakes) or Only from sender (a call sign, e.g. K6NLX).

Example: watching one net. To keep everything a net control station sends:

Name Region 6 net control · Watch messages from All applications · Keep Every message · Only from sender W5NCS.

Sharing forms

Export… saves all forms to a file; Import… adds forms from one, so a station can pass its forms to others.

Words also get added and removed by the app's own tools: Add as report… in Possible Misses can add a keyword to a topic, and Reports → Keyword Noise can stop one (Reliability tools). Changing forms re-checks the whole watch period.
