Exercises

S2 Watch recognises exercise and test traffic so a drill never passes for a real event.

Exercise in Progress

Configuration → Exercise in Progress tells S2 Watch an exercise is running.

Off (the default)On
Exercise messagesOnly in Live Monitor and History (purple rows, “EXERCISE:”); hidden from the map, the Events and Reports lists, Needs Review and notices Judged like real traffic: news lists, events, map, alerts

Switching it on re-checks the watch period, so injects already received are picked up. Turn it off after ENDEX: exercise traffic then leaves the news lists and events. Exercise messages never count toward station track records or keyword noise.

Seeing exercise traffic

During an exercise, exercise traffic is purple everywhere, a colour nothing else in S2 Watch uses (concern levels are red, orange and yellow). Outside one, all of this is hidden except the purple rows in Live Monitor and History.

  • By the clock: a purple EXERCISE · 2 button counts the exercise messages in the watch period (a digest split into several items counts once). Click it for the Events tab showing only exercise events.
  • Events lists (the Events tab and the list under the map): the row is tinted purple, the title starts EXERCISE ·, and the Concern column reads Exercise (its tooltip gives the level it would have were it real). Tick Exercise only on the Events tab to list just these (it is greyed out outside an exercise); it is cleared when S2 Watch restarts, so it can't hide real events for long.
  • Event panel: a purple EXERCISE EXERCISE EXERCISE bar at the top, and a purple EXERCISE tag on each exercise report.
  • Map: exercise markers have a purple ring and an EX tag; the popup starts with an EXERCISE bar.
  • Reports list and Live Monitor: exercise rows are tinted purple.
  • Needs Review: each exercise message of the last 24 hours is listed as Exercise traffic; a digest is one item, Exercise digest — …, and Open selects all its events.
  • Notices: new exercise traffic is announced in the status bar and kept under the 🔔; desktop alerts for it say EXERCISE.

What counts as exercise traffic

Radio messages only (JS8Call, MagCommstat, Commstat, FLMSG, JS8SuperSpotter, VarAC, APRS), and only with clear markers. Capital letters don't matter.

Recognised as exercise traffic:

W1AW: @MAGNET EXERCISE EXERCISE EXERCISE DAM FAILURE AT LAKE X
EXERCISE: TORNADO DAMAGE IN TULSA
THIS IS A DRILL. BRIDGE COLLAPSE ON I-35
EXERCISE MESSAGE 3 OF 5 - FLOODING
FOR EXERCISE PURPOSES ONLY - SHELTER OPEN AT ADA HS
SIMULATED EMERGENCY: CHEMICAL SPILL ON RT 66
STARTEX 1400Z
NOT REAL WORLD - POWER OUT IN OKC
**** EXERCIZE **** POWER AND COMMUNICATIONS FAILURE   (a banner, even misspelled)
EXERCISE                                               (the word alone on its own line)
EXERCISE PRIORITY ITEMS:                               (a line led by the word)

Common misspellings count too: EXERCIZE, EXCERCISE, EXERSIZE. A banner is the word between two or more *, =, # or ~, or three or more dashes.

Treated as real traffic:

REAL WORLD: EXERCISE SUSPENDED, ACTUAL TORNADO IN TULSA     (real world wins)
NO DUFF NO DUFF EXERCISE EXERCISE INJURED PERSON AT SITE    (no duff wins)
Report actual events (not exercise or test)                 (no marker)
Exercise caution on I-40 after the storm                    (a lone word in a sentence)
State Dept: Kenya - Level 1: Exercise Normal Precautions    (official feeds never count)

Test traffic (never news):

THIS IS A TEST OF THE NET
RFI test message
TEST TEST DE W1AW

A line led by the word counts unless the next word makes it ordinary English: “Exercise caution…”, “Exercise normal precautions…”, “Exercise is…”, “Exercise your right…”.

Digests (a weekly snapshot or bulletin split into its numbered items): when the message is marked, every item is exercise traffic, though the items themselves don't repeat the word. Each item is its own event, as it would be for real traffic. Only a digest is split: an inject about one incident written as numbered points (a SITREP) stays one event.

Drills and real traffic never mix. An exercise copy of a real message is kept as its own report, and exercise reports form their own events: a drill can't be folded into a real event, or a real event made to look like a drill.

Tips for exercise planners

  • Start every inject with EXERCISE EXERCISE EXERCISE. A message with no marker looks like real traffic.
  • In FLMSG forms, put the marker in the subject or message text: a field that just says “EXERCISE” isn't enough.
  • An inject must still be kept by a watch form: one with news words (flood, outage, injured…) is kept by the Topics; for injects without them, tick that application's “All … messages” form for the exercise.
