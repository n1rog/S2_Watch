The map

Markers sit at each report's place, coloured by category; clicking one selects its event. Reports placed only by state or MARS region sit at its centre. Map filters (above the map) chooses what is shown:

LayerShows
Map icon legendWhat each category's colour and symbol mean
Weather radarThe last 2 hours as a loop
NWS watches & warningsActive alert areas by severity; click one for its text
Station coverageGrid squares stations were heard from, and when
Day / night (grey line)Where it is dark, for HF propagation
Aircraft (ADS-B)Civilian (yellow), military (blue), medevac (red); emergencies (squawk 7500/7600/7700) get a black ring. Click an aircraft for its details and country of registration.
MARS regionsRegion outlines, focus regions bold
Online map tilesOpenStreetMap; off (or offline) uses the built-in base map
Source applications / categoriesHide markers from a source or a category

Example: saving data. The aircraft layer uses about 3–4 GB of internet data a day while civilian aircraft are shown. Untick Civilian (and Medevac if you don't need it) to keep only military and emergencies, or turn aircraft off.

If markers, aircraft or regions vanish while you zoom, tick Configuration → Display… → Software map rendering and restart S2 Watch: some graphics drivers (seen with Intel Iris Xe) drop them. If the map's engine is stopped, for example when the computer runs out of memory, S2 Watch reloads the map by itself and says so in the status bar.
