The map

Markers sit at each report's place, coloured by category; clicking one selects its event. Reports placed only by state or MARS region sit at its centre. The list under the map is resizable: drag the bar between them (it has a grip in the middle and highlights under the mouse) up for a bigger list or down for a bigger map; S2 Watch remembers where you leave it. United States and World (above the map) center it on the lower 48 states or zoom out to the whole world. Map filters (above the map) chooses what is shown:

Example: saving data. The aircraft layer uses about 3–4 GB of internet data a day while civilian aircraft are shown. Untick Civilian (and Medevac if you don't need it) to keep only military and emergencies, or turn aircraft off.

To follow particular aircraft, type them in the box beside Civilian, Military or Medevac: tail numbers, callsigns or ICAO addresses, separated by commas or spaces. That type then shows only those aircraft; an empty box shows them all. * matches anything, so REACH* is every REACH flight. Capitals and dashes don't matter (c-gabc is C-GABC). Emergencies still show, and the list changes only what the map shows, not the data fetched.

Example. Military REACH*, PACK21 · Medevac N911LF, LIFE* · Civilian empty: only REACH flights and PACK21 among military aircraft, only N911LF and LIFE… flights among air ambulances, and all civilian traffic.

If markers, aircraft or regions vanish while you zoom, tick Configuration → Display… → Software map rendering and restart S2 Watch: some graphics drivers (seen with Intel Iris Xe) drop them. If the map's engine is stopped, for example when the computer runs out of memory, S2 Watch reloads the map by itself and says so in the status bar.
