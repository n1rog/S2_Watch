Reports and briefings

S2 Watch drafts MAGNET S2 Intelligence Reports. Nothing is sent: you edit, then copy or save.

The draft window

  1. 1. Full report: the MAGNET S2 report. Write and edit here first. Lines in (parentheses) are prompts to replace.
  2. 2. FLMSG: upper-case text wrapped for radio, made from the full report as you edit; Save as text file… for an FLMSG form.
  3. 3. JS8Call: a short message from the report's fields, with a character count.

Buttons: Suggest assessment & gaps (Ollama) if a local model is set up; Copy this tab; Save Word report… and Save web page… in our format; Save in S2 Watch to reopen later from Reports → Saved Reports & Briefings…; and Mark report “Follow-up created” / Mark briefing sent.

What the report holds

The routing table (Subject, Purpose, DTG, Reporting Period, Geographic Focus, Precedence, MagCon Status, Sources), then Summary (BLUF), Background, Situation with a timeline, Comments / Assessment with a probability table, Intelligence Gaps, Mitigation Recommendations, MAGNET Guidance, the Source List and tags. S2 Watch fills what its data supports; the analysis sections start as prompts.

Background is filled with facts S2 Watch has, then a short prompt for what only you know (what is normal for the area or hazard, the basis for the MagCon status):

  • Follow-up report: the NWS watches and warnings for the event's kind of hazard in effect at its location (or that none are); earlier related events in the previous 30 days (same kind of hazard, within 150 km or in the same state) and how they ended; FEMA declarations for the state in the last 30 days declared for that hazard. Only sources related to the report appear: a report on a flood lists flood alerts, not a wind advisory, and a report that isn't about a weather hazard (a shooting, a power outage, a protest) has no NWS or FEMA lines at all.
  • Shift briefing: standing conditions (active NWS watches and warnings by severity and type, marine ones aside); ongoing situations that began before the period; FEMA declarations in the last 30 days; space weather; the MagCon status; the coverage, when beyond the U.S.

Example: a follow-up on a Florida flood.

BACKGROUND
NWS watches and warnings for this hazard in effect at the location: Coastal Flood Advisory (until 25 Oct 0900Z);
Coastal Flood Warning (until 25 Oct 0900Z).
Earlier related activity in the previous 30 days: 21 Oct: Flood — Florida ×8 (20 reports, confirmed by NWS).
FEMA declarations for Florida in the last 30 days: Hurricane Isaias — Florida; incident type Tropical Storm;
27 areas designated (09 Oct).
(Add what is normal for this area or hazard.)

Example: a follow-up on a shooting. No weather or FEMA lines, only S2 Watch's own record.

BACKGROUND
S2 Watch recorded no similar events within 150 km or in the same state in the previous 30 days.
(Add what is normal for this area or hazard.)

In the Word and web versions, “time | event” lines become the timeline and “category | HIGH” lines the probability table. Files are named from the subject and DTG, e.g. MAGNET_S2_FLOODING_TULSA_OK_261023-1430Z.

Source ratings

Each source gets a suggested Admiralty rating: reliability A for official sources, C for news, and for a radio station the grade its track record suggests (F until it can be judged); credibility 1 when another independent source confirms it, 2 when several agree, 6 when it can't yet be judged. Adjust them as the analyst.

What briefings include

  • priority items: your focus regions and custom area first, then by urgency, radio-reported events ahead of official-only ones;
  • for each, any warning: single source, quiet for some hours, radio silence nearby;
  • activity by category; events closed in the period under Resolved in the Period;
  • what is new since the last briefing marked sent.

The MagCon status you set in a report becomes the default for the next one. During an exercise, drafts are marked EXERCISE at the top, bottom and in the subject (Exercises).
