Bomb Threat at Kraft Heinz Plant, Columbia, Missouri

BLUF: A bomb threat emptied the Kraft Heinz plant in Columbia, MO on 15 Nov; police say there is no public danger and no device has been reported. Police left the scene within about 2.5 hours. No source links this threat to the Peoria, IL bombing two days earlier.

Precedence: RR – ROUTINE · DTG: 261116-0455Z · MagCon: 3 – ELEVATED · Geographic focus: Columbia, Missouri (Boone County)

Background
The Kraft Heinz plant, also called the Kraft Heinz/Oscar Mayer facility, is at 4600 Waco Road, Columbia, MO. [W1], [W2]
Columbia police often handle bomb threats that turn out to be hoaxes. One example: a threat at the Conley Road Walmart in April 2026, where bomb technicians found no explosives. [W4]
Two days earlier, on 13 Nov, the FBI confirmed that a real explosive device was found and made safe at the BioUrja ethanol plant in Peoria, IL. Most bomb threats are not legitimate; Peoria was the exception. [W5]
Situation

Mitigation Recommendations
Plant and site operators: follow police direction and your site bomb-threat procedures. Record exactly how and when any threat arrives.
Follow official Columbia Police Department channels for road reopenings and updates.
Industrial and food-plant security staff: keep reporting threats to local police. Peoria shows that some threats are real.
